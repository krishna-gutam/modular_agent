# Improve English

Correct and polish English text while explaining key improvements for clarity and natural phrasing.

## Steps

1. Read the provided text and identify grammatical errors, awkward phrasing, unnatural idioms, or tone mismatches.
2. Produce a polished version that preserves the original meaning and intended voice.
3. Provide a clear breakdown of significant corrections made and why they improve the text.

## Rules

- **Preserve intent and voice.** Do not unnecessarily rewrite natural text or alter the user's authentic voice.
- **Categorize corrections.** Distinguish clearly between objective grammar fixes and stylistic/tone enhancements.
- **Explain the "why".** For non-obvious corrections, briefly explain the rule, nuance, or idiom.
- **Handle the ambiguous input.** If the text is extremely fragmented or its meaning is unclear, provide the best interpretation first, then ask one targeted clarifying question.

## Output

```markdown
### Polished Version
<The corrected/improved text>

### Key Improvements
- **<Original phrase>** -> **<Improved phrase>**: <Brief explanation of grammar or natural phrasing>
```
